Privacy Policy - Bison

Last updated: October 2026

Bison ("the App") is an internal content-management and publishing tool operated by its developer to research, produce, schedule, and publish video content to Facebook Pages that the developer's own Business Portfolio owns and manages. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Who operates this App

Bison is operated as a single-business internal tool. It is not distributed to the public and is not used to access or manage Facebook Pages, accounts, or data belonging to anyone outside the operator's own Business Portfolio.

2. What data the App accesses

Through the Facebook Graph API, the App accesses only the following, and only for Pages the operator's own Business Portfolio owns:

  • Page identity information (Page ID, Page name) needed to address publishing requests correctly.
  • Page access tokens, used solely to authorize publishing and read-back requests made by the App on the Page's behalf.
  • Page content the App itself creates and publishes (video files, captions, scheduling metadata).
  • Page-level performance insights (views, reach, watch time, reactions, comments, shares) for content the App has published, used to measure and improve that content.

The App does not access or collect data about individual people who view, follow, comment on, or react to a Page's posts, beyond the aggregate counts described above.

3. How data is used

All data described above is used exclusively to: (a) publish and schedule video content to the operator's own Pages, and (b) measure the real-world performance of that content so future content can be improved. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising targeting.

4. Data storage and retention

Page access tokens are not persisted to long-term storage; the App derives a fresh, short-lived Page token from its Business System User credentials at the time of each request. Content metadata and performance-insight snapshots are stored in a private, operator-controlled database and are retained only as long as needed for the operator's own content-performance analysis. The operator may delete this data at any time.

5. Data sharing

No data accessed by this App is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The App does not integrate with any advertising, analytics, or data-broker service beyond Meta's own Graph API.

6. Security

Access to the App's credentials and underlying systems is restricted to the operator. Industry-standard practices (access tokens scoped to the minimum required permissions, no plaintext credential storage, encrypted connections for all API calls) are used throughout.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent revision.
